# Operator (Processor) Agreement — Clause Checklist
### A free starter checklist from Sentinel Privacy · sentinelprivacy.co.za

> General starter guidance, **not legal advice** and not a contract. Use it to
> sense-check agreements with third parties who process personal information on
> your behalf. For drafting/reviewing agreements, contact sentinelprivacy.co.za

---

Under POPIA, when a third party (an "operator") processes personal information
on your behalf — payroll, cloud hosting, IT support, a marketing agency — you
must have a **written contract** in place. Check that it covers:

- [ ] The operator processes personal information **only** on your documented instructions
- [ ] The operator treats the information as **confidential**
- [ ] The operator applies appropriate **security safeguards** (POPIA s19)
- [ ] The operator notifies you **immediately** of any security compromise (breach)
- [ ] Restrictions on **sub-processors** (and your right to approve them)
- [ ] Any **cross-border transfer** is identified and safeguarded (POPIA s72)
- [ ] The operator assists you in **responding to data-subject requests**
- [ ] The operator **returns or deletes** personal information at the end of the service
- [ ] Your right to **audit / obtain assurance** of compliance
- [ ] Clear **roles**: you are the responsible party, they are the operator
- [ ] **Liability and indemnity** for data-protection failures

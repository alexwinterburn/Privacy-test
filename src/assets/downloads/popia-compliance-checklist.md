## 1. Accountability & governance
- [ ] Leadership has formally acknowledged POPIA as a priority
- [ ] A person is accountable for data protection
- [ ] Information Officer appointed
- [ ] Information Officer (and deputies) registered with the Information Regulator
- [ ] A written, approved data-protection / POPIA policy exists

## 2. Know your data
- [ ] A personal-information inventory (data map) exists and is kept current
- [ ] We know what we collect, why, where it's stored and who can access it
- [ ] We only collect what is adequate, relevant and necessary (minimality)
- [ ] A lawful basis is recorded for each processing activity

## 3. Transparency
- [ ] A plain-language privacy notice is published on our website
- [ ] Privacy notices appear at each point where we collect personal information
- [ ] Notices explain what we collect, why, sharing, retention and data-subject rights

## 4. Data-subject rights
- [ ] A documented process exists to handle access, correction, deletion and objection requests
- [ ] An owner and a response timeframe are defined
- [ ] Identity-verification steps are defined

## 5. Third parties (operators)
- [ ] All operators (processors) are listed
- [ ] Written operator agreements are in place with each
- [ ] Cross-border data flows are mapped and safeguarded

## 6. Security safeguards
- [ ] Access controls and strong authentication are in place
- [ ] Encryption is applied where appropriate
- [ ] Regular backups and a patching routine exist
- [ ] Safeguards are documented

## 7. Retention & quality
- [ ] Retention schedules are defined (delete/de-identify when no longer needed)
- [ ] Personal information is kept accurate and up to date

## 8. Breach readiness
- [ ] A written incident / breach-response plan exists
- [ ] A breach register is maintained
- [ ] The team knows how to notify the Regulator and affected people

## 9. People & awareness
- [ ] Staff have received POPIA awareness training in the last 12 months
- [ ] Privacy is included in onboarding
- [ ] A PAIA manual is prepared and available

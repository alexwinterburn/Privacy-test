## Privacy Notice for [BUSINESS NAME]

**Last updated:** [DATE]

[BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to
protecting your personal information in line with the Protection of Personal
Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

### 1. Who we are
We are the responsible party for your personal information. Our Information
Officer can be contacted at [EMAIL] / [PHONE].

### 2. What personal information we collect
We may collect: [e.g. name, contact details, ID number, payment details,
information you provide when you contact us]. 

### 3. Why we collect it (purpose)
We process your personal information to: [e.g. provide our products/services,
process orders and payments, respond to enquiries, meet legal obligations,
and — where you have consented — send you marketing].

### 4. Our lawful basis
We rely on: [consent / performance of a contract / legal obligation /
legitimate interests / protection of your interests], depending on the activity.

### 5. Who we share it with
We may share your information with [operators/service providers such as
payment processors, hosting and IT providers, couriers], who are bound to
protect it. [State if any information is transferred outside South Africa and
the safeguards applied.]

### 6. How long we keep it
We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes above
or as required by law. [Insert retention approach.]

### 7. How we protect it
We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including
[access controls, encryption, backups].

### 8. Your rights
You have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to
processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with the Information
Regulator (inforegulator.org.za). To exercise your rights, contact us at [EMAIL].

### 9. Changes
We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version is always
available at [WEBSITE ADDRESS].

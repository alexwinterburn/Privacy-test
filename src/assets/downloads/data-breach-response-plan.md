## 3. Steps

**Step 1 — Identify & report (immediately)**
Anyone who suspects a breach reports it to the Information Officer at once.

**Step 2 — Contain**
Stop the compromise: isolate affected systems, revoke access, reset credentials,
preserve evidence and logs.

**Step 3 — Assess**
Determine what happened, what personal information is involved, how many people
are affected and the likely risk to them. Record findings in the breach register.

**Step 4 — Notify**
Where there are reasonable grounds to believe personal information has been
accessed/acquired by an unauthorised person, notify:
- the **Information Regulator**, and
- the **affected data subjects**,
as soon as reasonably possible. Notification must be in writing and give enough
detail for people to protect themselves.

**Step 5 — Remediate & review**
Fix the root cause, document lessons learned and update controls and this plan.

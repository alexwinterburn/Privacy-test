Why POPIA matters

Non-compliance is a real, growing business risk

Since POPIA came into full force in July 2021, the Information Regulator has been actively enforcing it. Fines of up to R10 million, criminal liability and reputational damage are all on the table — but compliance is entirely achievable with the right guidance.

R10m

Maximum administrative fine for serious POPIA breaches.

10 yrs

Possible imprisonment for the most serious offences.

8

Conditions for lawful processing every business must meet.

72 hrs

The urgency expected when responding to a data breach.
